What does City mattress pickup cost around Boston? Here's what four cities' own pages say.

Boston's City page mentions no fee. It's for buildings with six units or fewer, curbside only. Book online or call 311.

Cambridge charges forty-five dollars per mattress and forty-five per box spring, through the City's contractor, UTEC. Curbside only.

Quincy is free for residents in homes, condos or buildings with eight units or fewer, through South Shore Recycle. Curbside only.

Somerville charges fifty-five dollars per mattress and fifty-five per box spring, since February first, 2026, for buildings with six or fewer units on City trash.

Massachusetts bans mattresses from the trash, so use one of these programs, a donation outlet, or a hauler.
