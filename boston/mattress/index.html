How to get rid of a mattress in Boston

Mattresses and box springs can't go out with regular trash in Boston, because Massachusetts bans them from disposal. Which option you use mostly depends on how many units your building has.

Last verified 2026-10-26 · page updated 2026-10-29

Can I throw a mattress in the trash in Boston?No. Massachusetts bans mattresses and box springs from disposal, so they can't go out with regular trash in Boston.
How do I schedule City of Boston mattress removal?Book a curbside pickup online on the City's Mattress Recycling page, or call 311 (617-635-4500). It's for buildings with 6 units or fewer, by appointment only, and the City page doesn't mention a fee. Put it at the curb by 6 a.m. on your appointment day (or after 5 p.m. the night before). To cancel, use the link in your confirmation email or call 311 before 12 p.m. the day before.
What if my building has 7 or more units, and what do mattress haulers charge?The City does not collect mattresses from buildings with more than six units. Ask your property manager or use a private hauler the City lists. Prices on the City's list include Tough Stuff Recycling $75 per pickup, Burns Cleanout $100, Lug Away $124 curbside or $174 from inside the home, and CC Junk Removal $125 with indoor removal included. The others quote on request.
What if the mattress is soiled or has bed bugs?For the City pickup, bag and seal it completely. Don't cut or crush it.
Can I donate a used mattress in Boston?Some furniture banks take clean mattresses with no stains or rips, such as Furnishing Hope and Household Goods. Many donation outlets, including the Habitat Greater Boston ReStore, don't take mattresses at all.

Building with 6 units or fewer: book the City pickup

The City of Boston collects mattresses and box springs curbside by appointment only, for buildings with 6 units or fewer. Put it at the curb by 6 a.m. on your appointment day (or after 5 p.m. the night before). If it's soiled or has bed bugs, bag and seal it completely. Don't cut or crush it. Each address can have up to 10 items a calendar year. Book the pickup online on the City's Mattress Recycling page, or call 311 (617-635-4500). To cancel, use the link in your confirmation or reminder email, or call 311, before 12 p.m. the day before. If it isn't collected on your date, call 311 within 24 hours or you'll have to reschedule.

Building with 7 or more units

The City does not collect mattresses from buildings with more than six units. Ask your property manager, or use one of the private mattress haulers the City lists (below). The City's list gives prices for some of them: Tough Stuff Recycling $75 per mattress or box spring for pickup ($25 to drop off, with proof of Boston residency or business), Burns Cleanout & Junk Removal $100 per mattress, Lug Away Junk Removal $124 curbside or $174 from inside the home ($245 for a king), CC Junk Removal & Hauling $125 per mattress or box spring with indoor removal included, and HandUp Mattress Recycling $35 to drop off. The others quote on request.

Donating a mattress

Some furniture banks take clean mattresses with no stains or rips, such as Furnishing Hope and Household Goods below. Many donation outlets, including the Habitat Greater Boston ReStore, don't take mattresses at all.

Buying a new one?

The City suggests asking the seller whether it will take your old mattress for recycling when it delivers the new one.
