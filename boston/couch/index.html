How to get rid of a couch or sofa in Boston

Unlike mattresses, a couch doesn't need a special appointment in Boston: the City says furniture can go out with your normal curbside trash and recycling. If it's still in good shape, a few local furniture banks and charities will take it, but several donation outlets refuse sofas, so check the rules below first.

What is the cheapest way to dispose of a sofa in Boston?Put it out with your regular trash. The City of Boston says furniture can go out with your normal curbside trash and recycling on your scheduled collection day, and its page doesn't mention a fee (call 311 if you're unsure). It must be at the curb by 6 a.m., not before 5 p.m. the evening before.
Can I put a couch out with the trash in Boston?Yes. The City of Boston says furniture can go out with your normal curbside trash and recycling on your scheduled collection day, placed neatly next to your trash barrels, with no special appointment. It must be at the curb by 6 a.m., and not before 5 p.m. the evening before.
How much does couch removal cost in Boston?City curbside pickup has no fee mentioned on the City page, but it's curbside only. Paid services that come inside list starting prices: LoadUp lists couch removal from $85 on its national page (final price confirmed by ZIP code, item, and pickup conditions), and ReSupply's Boston page lists sofas and bulky furniture starting at $99 plus a separate booking fee.
Will the City take a couch from inside my apartment?No. The City picks up curbside only, not from inside your home or on private property. Paid services such as ReSupply (donation pickup) and LoadUp (junk removal) can come inside; get the final price before booking.
Where can I donate a couch near Boston?Household Goods in Acton lists couches, sofa beds, and futons that are clean, complete, and not torn or stained. Furnishing Hope of Massachusetts accepts sofas with no stains, odors, pet hair, or major wear, but its pickup requires at least six substantial furniture items and it requests a donation.
Does the Habitat ReStore in Boston take sofas?The Habitat for Humanity Greater Boston ReStore does not accept sofa beds or sofas with slip covers, and other upholstered furniture can't be ripped, worn, broken, dated, or faded. The Thrift Shop of Boston does not accept couches.
Will a furniture store take my old couch when it delivers a new one? (Room & Board, IKEA, Bob's, Jordan's)Only some do, and only if you arrange it when you buy. Bob's Discount Furniture sells removal with a new-furniture delivery ($150 for the first piece, $50 each additional). IKEA offers sofa removal from $30 with an in-store sofa purchase and in-home delivery in select markets, not with online orders. Jordan's Furniture says its delivery team cannot move or remove existing furniture. Room & Board's delivery and FAQ pages describe assembly, placement and packaging removal and don't mention taking old furniture. Checked Oct 29, 2026.

Free option: put it out on trash day

The City of Boston says furniture can be put out with your normal curbside trash and recycling on your scheduled collection day, placed neatly next to your trash barrels. Trash must be at the curb by 6 a.m., and not before 5 p.m. the evening before. The City picks up curbside only, not from inside your home or on private property. The City page doesn't mention a fee; call 311 if you're unsure. If you live in a building with 7 or more units, check the City's large-building page and ask your property manager.

Couch removal cost in Boston: free curbside vs. paid pickup

Curbside on trash day: the City of Boston page doesn't mention a fee, but the City will not pick up on private property, so you have to get the couch to the curb yourself. LoadUp: its national junk removal page lists couch removal from $85 (starting price; the final price is confirmed by ZIP code, item variation, and pickup conditions) and a full-service option that removes items from inside the home. ReSupply: its Boston page lists sofas, bulky furniture, and room-sized loads starting at $99, with pricing that varies by location, items, availability, and handling needs, plus a separate booking fee that is non-refundable unless ReSupply can't complete the pickup as requested. Furnishing Hope doesn't charge a pickup fee but suggests a tax-deductible donation of $250 to $400 depending on the size of the donation, and only picks up six or more substantial furniture items.

Donating a couch that's in good shape

Household Goods in Acton lists couches, sofa beds, and futons among the furniture it accepts, as long as items are clean, complete, and not torn or stained. Furnishing Hope of Massachusetts accepts sofas and upholstered chairs with no stains, odors, pet hair, or major wear, but its pickup requires at least six substantial furniture items and it requests a donation. The Salvation Army's national pickup FAQ lists sofas, though it says accepted items vary by location, so call 800-SA-TRUCK.

Who won't take a couch

The Habitat for Humanity Greater Boston ReStore accepts some upholstered furniture (not ripped, worn, broken, dated, or faded) but does not accept sofa beds or sofas with slip covers. The Thrift Shop of Boston does not accept couches, futons, or sleep sofas, or chairs with upholstery stains, tears, or sun damage.

Will the furniture store take your old couch? Room & Board, IKEA, Bob's and Jordan's

Only if you set it up when you buy, and not every store offers it. Bob's Discount Furniture sells a furniture removal service only with delivery of new Bob's furniture: $150 for the first piece and $50 for each additional piece (its example: a 3-piece sectional is $250). It has to be added at purchase, can't be added once delivery is scheduled, and Bob's says it may be unavailable in some areas. IKEA offers sofa removal starting at $30 when you buy a sofa or similar product (sectional, loveseat, futon) in-store with in-home delivery in select markets; it isn't available with online purchases, and removal may not happen at the same time as the delivery. Jordan's Furniture says its delivery team cannot move or remove existing furniture; it does offer removal of old bedding for a small fee with mattress deliveries. Room & Board's delivery and FAQ pages describe its $159 full-service delivery as assembly, placement and packaging removal and don't mention taking away old furniture, so ask Room & Board before you order. We checked these store pages on Oct 29, 2026. If your store won't take it, the City's trash-day option above is still free.

Sources: Bob's: furniture removal service · IKEA US: removal services · Jordan's: delivery & pickup · Room & Board: delivery · Room & Board: FAQ

Need someone to carry it out?

The City won't come inside, so if your couch is upstairs you'll need help. ReSupply arranges paid donation pickups in Boston, with teams that go up and down stairs; its Boston page lists sofas and bulky furniture starting at $99, plus a separate booking fee, with pricing varying by location and items. LoadUp, a junk removal service, lists couch removal from $85 on its national page and offers an inside-the-home option. Get the final price before booking.
