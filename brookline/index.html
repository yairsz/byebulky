How to get rid of a couch, mattress or furniture in Brookline

Brookline collects big items at the curb for a fee if you're on Town trash service. Book and pay a week ahead online or at Town Hall: $20 for furniture or a rug, $30 for an appliance or electronics, $65 for each mattress or box spring. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

How do I schedule a bulky item pickup in Brookline?Register on the Town's Accela permit portal and request a pickup, or pay in person at Town Hall. Book and pay at least one week before collection; up to four items per week.
How much is bulky item pickup in Brookline, MA?$20 per special item such as wood or upholstered furniture or a rug, $30 per electronic or white good, and $65 per mattress or box spring. Online card payments carry a service fee.
What day is bulk trash pickup in Brookline?Furniture put out as trash, like desks and chairs, goes on your regular trash day. Mattresses, electronics, metal and white goods are collected on Tuesdays. Your confirmation email gives each item's date.
What won't Brookline pick up?Construction or remodel material, car parts, gym equipment and pianos, plus apartment, attic or basement clean-outs. The Town says to use a private permitted waste hauler for those.
Does bulk pickup take mattresses in Brookline?Not with regular bulky item pickup. Brookline's furniture pickup listing names this as not accepted: "Mattresses and box springs ($65 each, see the mattress listing)." The official mattress option is Town of Brookline mattress & box spring pickup ($65 per item). Cost: $65 per mattress or box spring. When: Mattresses are collected on Tuesdays each week; your confirmation email gives the date.

City bulk item pickup for Brookline residents (3)

city bulk pickup

Town of Brookline bulky item pickup ($20 per special item)

Takes
  • Wood or upholstered furniture, such as a couch
  • Rugs and carpet
  • Desks and chairs (collected on your regular trash day)
Not accepted
  • Construction or remodel material
  • Car parts
  • Gym equipment
  • Pianos
  • Mattresses and box springs ($65 each, see the mattress listing)
  • Electronics and white goods ($30 each, see that listing)
  • Apartment, attic or basement clean-outs (Town says use a private permitted hauler)
Condition rules
Book and pay at least one week before pickup, online through the Town's Accela permit portal or in person at Town Hall (333 Washington St., 4th floor). Up to four bulky, metal, white goods and/or mattress items per week. Refund requests must be made 48 hours in advance; service fees aren't refunded and refunds come as a mailed check. Your confirmation email gives the collection day for each item.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside collection only.
Lead time / hours
Schedule one week in advance. Items disposed of as trash, such as desks and chairs, are collected on your regular trash day.
Cost
$20 per special item (wood or upholstered furniture, rugs/carpet, etc.). Pay before pickup; online card payments carry a service fee that isn't refunded.
Service area
Brookline residents on Town Waste Collection Service.
How to book
Website / booking · 617-730-2156 · dpwinfo@brooklinema.gov
Notes
The Town encourages donating or selling furniture and appliances in good condition first.

drop-off

Brookline Household Hazardous Waste drop-off, Transfer Station (815 Newton St.)

Takes
  • Tires
  • TVs, CRTs and other electronics
  • Small appliances such as microwaves
  • Air conditioners
  • Household hazardous waste in original labeled containers (oil-based paint, solvents, pesticides, propane tanks, car batteries, motor oil and more)
Not accepted
  • Commercial or workplace waste
  • Hazardous waste in non-original or unlabeled containers
  • Furniture and mattresses (not listed by the Town for this site)
Condition rules
Brookline residents only: bring ID and proof of residency (utility bill, lease or government ID). Come by car. One trunkload per resident per week; for more, email zerowaste@brooklinema.gov for an appointment.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. You bring the items.
Lead time / hours
Open May through October, 7:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. The Bulky Items page says every Tuesday; the hazardous waste pages say every other Tuesday, so check before you go.
Cost
Free for tires, electronics and small appliances, per the Town's Bulky Items page.
Service area
Brookline residents only.
Address
815 Newton Street, Brookline (Chestnut Hill), MA
How to book
Website / booking · 617-730-2156 · zerowaste@brooklinema.gov
Notes
Seasonal: the Town lists it as open May through October only.

Utility appliance recycling programs for Brookline residents (1)

utility recycling program

Mass Save refrigerator and freezer recycling ($75 rebate)

Takes
  • Working refrigerators (inside size 10 to 30 cubic feet)
  • Working freezers (inside size 10 to 30 cubic feet)
  • Working dehumidifiers ($30 rebate, only with a fridge or freezer pickup)
Not accepted
  • Refrigerators or freezers that don't work (must be plugged in and cooling at pickup)
  • Units smaller than 10 or larger than 30 cubic feet inside
Condition rules
Must be an inefficient, working unit that is plugged in, cooling, and empty at pickup; disconnect any water line first. You must own it and it must be at your residence or business. All doors must be removed before placing it outside. Maximum two units per equipment type per electric account per calendar year. Only for Massachusetts electric customers whose Mass Save Sponsor is Cape Light Compact, Eversource, National Grid or Unitil.
Stairs / inside pickup
Inside pickup only where there is one flight of stairs or fewer (per Mass Save); with more stairs you must make the item accessible for outside pickup.
Lead time / hours
Not stated, check with them. Rebate processing takes 6 to 8 weeks after pickup.
Cost
No-cost pickup plus a $75 rebate (pickup must happen between January 1 and December 31, 2026).
Service area
Massachusetts electric customers whose Mass Save Sponsor is Cape Light Compact, Eversource, National Grid or Unitil. Check that your electric utility is one of these.
How to book
Website / booking · 866-527-SAVE (7283)
Notes
Have your electric account number handy when scheduling, per Mass Save.
