How to get rid of a couch or sofa in Brookline

If you're on Brookline's Town trash service, the cheapest way to get rid of a couch is the Town's bulky item pickup: $20 per special item, booked and paid a week ahead, collected at the curb. If the couch is still in good shape you can donate it, and if you need it carried out of an upstairs apartment you'll need a paid hauler.

Quick answers: couch in Brookline

Does Brookline pick up couches?

Yes, for residents on Town Waste Collection Service. A couch counts as a special item (wood or upholstered furniture), which costs $20. Book and pay one week in advance, online through the Town's Accela permit portal or in person at Town Hall.

Source: Town of Brookline: bulky items and overflow waste

How much does it cost to get rid of a couch in Brookline?

The Town charges $20 per special item such as a couch, and online card payments carry a service fee. LoadUp's Brookline price list shows furniture removal, including sofas, from $120, with the final price shown by ZIP before booking.

Source: Town of Brookline: bulky items and overflow waste · LoadUp

How do I schedule a bulky item pickup for a couch in Brookline?

Create an account on the Town's Accela permit portal, then request a bulky item pickup, or schedule and pay in person at Town Hall, 333 Washington St., 4th floor. Your confirmation email gives the collection date. For help, email dpwinfo@brooklinema.gov or call DPW at 617-730-2156.

Source: Town of Brookline: bulky items and overflow waste

Will someone take a couch out of my apartment in Brookline?

Not the Town, which collects at the curb. LoadUp says its loaders haul away sofas, futons and sectionals from anywhere on your property. The Town won't take apartment clean-outs and points residents to private permitted waste haulers for those.

Source: Town of Brookline: bulky items and overflow waste · LoadUp · Town of Brookline: private waste haulers

Where can I donate a couch near Brookline?

The Habitat Greater Boston ReStore in West Roxbury takes upholstered furniture that isn't ripped, worn, broken, dated or faded, but not sofa beds or sofas with slip covers. Household Goods in Acton takes couches, sofa beds and futons that are clean and in good condition, at its drop-off or through paid pickup partners.

Source: Habitat for Humanity Greater Boston ReStore · Household Goods

The $20 Town pickup: who qualifies and how to book

For residents on Town Waste Collection Service, the Department of Public Works collects up to four bulky, metal, white goods and/or mattress items per week. Couches are listed as bulky items, and wood or upholstered furniture is charged as a special item at $20. Pickups must be scheduled one week in advance and paid before pickup, online through the Town's Accela permit portal (register an account first) or in person at Town Hall. Your confirmation email gives the collection day for each item. For help, email dpwinfo@brooklinema.gov or call DPW at 617-730-2156.

Changing your plans: the 48-hour refund rule

Refund requests must be made 48 hours in advance, for in-person and online payments alike. Service fees aren't included in the refund, and refunds are sent as a check in the mail.

Moving out or clearing a whole apartment

The Town won't collect apartment, attic or basement clean-outs, and it doesn't accept construction or remodel material, car parts, gym equipment or pianos through bulky pickup. For those, Brookline points residents to private permitted waste haulers; its linked list is dated August 2023, and each hauler sets its own price.

Paid couch removal from inside your home

LoadUp says its loaders haul away sofas, futons and sectionals from anywhere on your property. Its Brookline junk removal page says pickups start at $105, with additional items from +$15, and its Brookline price list shows furniture removal (sofas, dressers, bed frames, tables) from $120. An older LoadUp Brookline bulky item page still says a base rate of $80, so confirm the price for your ZIP when you book.

Donating a sofa in good condition

Brookline encourages residents to donate or sell furniture in good condition first. The Habitat Greater Boston ReStore, 1580 VFW Parkway in West Roxbury, accepts upholstered furniture that isn't ripped, worn, broken, dated or faded, but not sofa beds or sofas with slip covers; drop-offs are accepted Tuesday through Saturday, 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Habitat points in-home pickups to ReSupply, but ReSupply's Boston page doesn't list Brookline among the towns shown, so check your address with ReSupply. Household Goods in Acton accepts couches, sofa beds and futons that are clean, complete and in good working condition; its volunteer pickup doesn't reach Brookline, so drop off at 530 Main Street on Tuesdays, Thursdays or Saturdays from 9 a.m. to noon, or use its paid pickup partner ReMARKable Donations.
