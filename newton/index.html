How to get rid of a couch, mattress or furniture in Newton

Newton doesn't take big items with the regular trash. You pay online for a curbside pickup, usually on Thursday of the following week: $20 for furniture, $60 for a mattress or box spring, $25 for an appliance or TV. Or bring it to the Resource Recovery Center on Rumford Avenue. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

Quick answers about bulky item pickup in Newton

Does Newton, MA have bulky item pickup?

Yes. Pay $20 per item online and the City arranges curbside collection, usually on Thursday of the following week if you pay by 10 p.m. Sunday. Set it out by 7 a.m. on collection day.

Source: City of Newton: large-item flyer

How much is mattress pickup in Newton, MA?

$60 per mattress or box spring for curbside collection, or $35 per item to drop it off yourself at the Resource Recovery Center, 115 Rumford Ave. Pay online first either way. Newton mattress removal and pickup guide.

Source: City of Newton: large-item flyer

How do I get rid of a refrigerator, washer or TV in Newton?

Book a whitegoods curbside pickup for $25 per item, or drop appliances and metal off free at the Resource Recovery Center. TVs and monitors cost $25 each to drop off. Newton fridge and appliance disposal guide.

Source: City of Newton: large-item flyer

What are the Newton dump (Rumford Ave. Resource Recovery Center) hours?

Winter hours, November 1 to March 27, are Monday to Friday 7 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. It's closed Sundays, holidays (including Wednesday, November 11 for Veterans Day) and sometimes after heavy rain or snow; call 617-796-1000 after a storm. It's at 115 Rumford Ave., Auburndale, for Newton residents only.

Source: City of Newton: Resource Recovery Center

Is there a bulk waste drop-off for Newton residents?

Yes, at the Resource Recovery Center, 115 Rumford Ave., for Newton residents only. It takes mattresses and box springs ($35 each), appliances, scrap metal and metal items at no cost, electronics free except TVs and monitors ($25 each), and large rigid plastic. It does not take couches; those use the $20 bulky pickup.

Source: Newton: Recycle at the Resource Recovery Center

What items won't Newton take as bulky waste?

Mattresses, box springs, appliances, metal, electronics, items over 150 lbs, cardboard, construction materials, and move-out or clean-out loads, which need a private hauler.

Source: City of Newton: managing household trash

What day is bulky item pickup in Newton?

Furniture & bulky items, mattresses & box springs and fridges & big appliances: For payments received by 10 p.m. Sunday, collection should be arranged for Thursday of the following week (unless there's a trash delay on Thursday).

City bulk item pickup for Newton residents (3)

city bulk pickup

City of Newton bulky item curbside pickup ($20 per item)

Takes
  • Any single non-recyclable item too large to fit in the blue trash cart, such as a couch (all sections of a sectional and its loose cushions count as one item, sleeper sofas included)
  • Wooden bed frames (headboard, footboard and rails count as one item)
  • Dismantled wooden furniture such as a bookshelf or desk (all pieces count as one item)
  • Wooden futon frames and futon mattresses (one item each)
  • Carpet and loose scrap wood tied in rolls or bundles no more than 4 feet long and 50 lbs (each counts as one item)
  • Vacuum cleaners and other mixed-material items not mostly metal; a pair of skis
Not accepted
  • Mattresses and box springs (separate $60 pickup, see below; banned from disposal since 11/1/2022)
  • Appliances, metal items, electronics and rigid plastic items (separate whitegoods pickup or free drop-off, see below)
  • Items over 150 lbs (need a private hauler)
  • Cardboard, overflow trash and construction materials
  • Move-outs and clean-outs (the City says these need a private hauler)
Condition rules
Pay online first ($20 per item). Set the item out by 7 a.m. on the collection day. Refunds only if you cancel and get a cancellation email by 10 a.m. the business day before collection; no refund if the item is scavenged from the curb after that deadline or isn't set out on time, and a new request and fee are needed if it wasn't out on time.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside collection only.
Lead time / hours
For payments received by 10 p.m. Sunday, collection should be arranged for Thursday of the following week (unless there's a trash delay on Thursday). You should get an email from Newton Customer Service within 1 to 2 business days and a reminder the business day before pickup.
Cost
$20 per item (City of Newton flyer, updated 8/17/2026).
Service area
Newton households on City trash collection. The City says businesses, institutions and many multi-family properties aren't entitled to City collection and must make private arrangements.
How to book
Website / booking · 617-796-1000
Notes
Fee, schedule and item-counting rules are from the City's "I have a large item I no longer want" flyer (updated 8.17.26) linked from the Managing Household Trash page.

Utility appliance recycling programs for Newton residents (1)

utility recycling program

Mass Save refrigerator and freezer recycling ($75 rebate)

Takes
  • Working refrigerators (inside size 10 to 30 cubic feet)
  • Working freezers (inside size 10 to 30 cubic feet)
  • Working dehumidifiers ($30 rebate, only with a fridge or freezer pickup)
Not accepted
  • Refrigerators or freezers that don't work (must be plugged in and cooling at pickup)
  • Units smaller than 10 or larger than 30 cubic feet inside
Condition rules
Must be an inefficient, working unit that is plugged in, cooling, and empty at pickup; disconnect any water line first. You must own it and it must be at your residence or business. All doors must be removed before placing it outside. Maximum two units per equipment type per electric account per calendar year. Only for Massachusetts electric customers whose Mass Save Sponsor is Cape Light Compact, Eversource, National Grid or Unitil.
Stairs / inside pickup
Inside pickup only where there is one flight of stairs or fewer (per Mass Save); with more stairs you must make the item accessible for outside pickup.
Lead time / hours
Not stated, check with them. Rebate processing takes 6 to 8 weeks after pickup.
Cost
No-cost pickup plus a $75 rebate (pickup must happen between January 1 and December 31, 2026).
Service area
Massachusetts electric customers whose Mass Save Sponsor is Cape Light Compact, Eversource, National Grid or Unitil. Check that your electric utility is one of these.
How to book
Website / booking · 866-527-SAVE (7283)
Notes
Have your electric account number handy when scheduling, per Mass Save.
