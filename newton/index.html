How to get rid of a couch, mattress or furniture in Newton

Newton doesn't take big items with the regular trash. You pay online for a curbside pickup, usually on Thursday of the following week: $20 for furniture, $60 for a mattress or box spring, $25 for an appliance or TV. Or bring it to the Resource Recovery Center on Rumford Avenue. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

City bulk item pickup for Newton residents (3)

city bulk pickup

City of Newton bulky item curbside pickup ($20 per item)

Takes
  • Any single non-recyclable item too large to fit in the blue trash cart, such as a couch (all sections of a sectional and its loose cushions count as one item, sleeper sofas included)
  • Wooden bed frames (headboard, footboard and rails count as one item)
  • Dismantled wooden furniture such as a bookshelf or desk (all pieces count as one item)
  • Wooden futon frames and futon mattresses (one item each)
  • Carpet and loose scrap wood tied in rolls or bundles no more than 4 feet long and 50 lbs (each counts as one item)
  • Vacuum cleaners and other mixed-material items not mostly metal; a pair of skis
Not accepted
  • Mattresses and box springs (separate $60 pickup, see below; banned from disposal since 11/1/2022)
  • Appliances, metal items, electronics and rigid plastic items (separate whitegoods pickup or free drop-off, see below)
  • Items over 150 lbs (need a private hauler)
  • Cardboard, overflow trash and construction materials
  • Move-outs and clean-outs (the City says these need a private hauler)
Condition rules
Pay online first ($20 per item). Set the item out by 7 a.m. on the collection day. Refunds only if you cancel and get a cancellation email by 10 a.m. the business day before collection; no refund if the item is scavenged from the curb after that deadline or isn't set out on time, and a new request and fee are needed if it wasn't out on time.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside collection only.
Lead time / hours
For payments received by 10 p.m. Sunday, collection should be arranged for Thursday of the following week (unless there's a trash delay on Thursday). You should get an email from Newton Customer Service within 1 to 2 business days and a reminder the business day before pickup.
Cost
$20 per item (City of Newton flyer, updated 8/17/2026).
Service area
Newton households on City trash collection. The City says businesses, institutions and many multi-family properties aren't entitled to City collection and must make private arrangements.
How to book
Website / booking · 617-796-1000
Notes
Fee, schedule and item-counting rules are from the City's "I have a large item I no longer want" flyer (updated 8.17.26) linked from the Managing Household Trash page.

Drop-off locations for Newton residents (3)

drop-off

Newton Resource Recovery Center drop-off, 115 Rumford Ave. (residents only)

Takes
  • Mattresses and box springs ($35 per item, prepaid)
  • Appliances, scrap metal and other metal items (no cost)
  • Electronics (no cost, except TVs and monitors at $25 each, prepaid)
  • Large rigid plastic items such as outdoor chairs and play structures
  • Textiles and clothing in donation bins; books at the Book Shed; white block Styrofoam, light bulbs, extra recycling
Not accepted
  • Bulky non-recyclable items such as couches (use the $20 bulky pickup)
  • Oil-based paints and stains (household hazardous waste events only)
  • Anything from businesses or commercial vehicles except cardboard and single-stream recycling
Condition rules
Newton residents only; proof of residency on request. For mattresses, box springs, TVs and monitors, pay online first and show proof of payment to the attendant; the number of items must match the receipt, and you unload mattresses yourself into the storage container.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. You bring the items.
Lead time / hours
Fall hours (September 28 to October 31): Monday to Saturday 7 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.; closed Sundays, holidays and sometimes after heavy rain. Hours after October 31 not stated yet, call 617-796-1000 before going.
Cost
Free for appliances, metal and most electronics. $35 per mattress or box spring; $25 per TV or monitor.
Service area
Newton residents only.
Address
115 Rumford Ave., Auburndale, MA 02466

Website / booking · 617-796-1000

furniture bank

Household Goods (Acton)

Takes
  • Standard-size mattresses & box springs
  • Bed frames (all parts) & bunk beds
  • Dressers & armoires
  • Couches, sofa beds & futons
  • Chairs (no office chairs)
  • Kitchen, dining, end & coffee tables
  • Refrigerators, washers, electric dryers (newer than 10 years)
  • Air conditioners up to 18" x 30" x 24" (newer than 10 years)
  • Flat-screen TVs under 55" (less than 10 years old)
Not accepted
  • Mattresses with any stains or rips
  • Office furniture, desks, file cabinets
  • Glass-top items
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Oversized or wall-mounted items
Condition rules
Clean, complete, and in good working condition; nothing broken, chipped, torn, stained, or recalled.
Stairs / inside pickup
Volunteer pickup asks that items be in a garage or on the first floor if possible.

Drop-off Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays, 9 a.m. to noon. Volunteer pickups typically scheduled 4+ weeks ahead. Paid pickups through ReSupply or ReMARKable Donations in as soon as 2 business days.

Volunteer pickup only in Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury and Westford, so not Newton. Newton donors can drop off in Acton or use the paid pickup partners (ReSupply or Mark's Moving/ReMARKable Donations).
