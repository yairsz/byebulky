Donate Furniture, Beds, and Household Items to Household Goods

Household Goods provides a full-range of good quality donated furniture and household items, free of charge, to 3,000 families in need each year.  We owe this to the incredible support of our community of volunteers and people and businesses who thoughtfully donate furniture and household items that meet our guidelines.

Our volunteers understand and share the frustration when they are faced with turning away items donors would like us to take. To avoid this situation, we strongly encourage all donors to read the guidelines carefully. Some of them may surprise you, but they reflect 30 years of experience in the service of our mission.

To make sure that we can accept your items, please first check the guidelines, then double check that your items are clean, complete and in good working order. We cannot provide our clients with broken, chipped, torn, stained, incomplete, unusable or recalled items, and therefore cannot accept them.

To furnish 50 homes a week, Household Goods needs a steady stream of clean, usable:

  • dressers, tables, chairs, and other furniture;
  • mattresses and bed frames;
  • dishes, flatware, and kitchen items;
  • sheets, towels, and other bed linens;
  • and other household items.

Please see our donation guidelines below; download our guidelines here. Please note that we do not accept clothing, food or toys.

We thank you in advance for your support in helping us help our community.

What We Can Accept

Click on each of the categories below to see what we can and cannot accept.

All items must be clean, complete, and in good working condition. We cannot afford to take and dispose of broken, chipped, torn, stained, incomplete, unusable or recalled items, or any item we deem unsuitable for our clients. Please contact us if you are uncertain about whether we will accept your item.

Furniture

  • Beds: Standard Sized Mattresses, Box Springs, Bed Frames and Bunk Beds (Bed Frames must include all parts and
    hardware)
  • Bureaus, Dressers, Armoires
  • Couches, Sofa Beds, Futons
  • Chairs (no office chairs or bean bags)
  • Tables: Kitchen, Dining, End, Coffee, Nightstands, TV Stands (NO Gaming, Glass or Drafting Tables)
  • Sideboards, Bookcases, Cabinets, Entertainment centers up to 6.5 ‘ tall

We cannot accept:

  • Mattresses with any stains or rips, Air Mattresses, Hospital, Water, Motorized or Sleep Number Beds, Loft Systems;
  • Oversized Items, Items with Glass Tops or Shelves, Wall Mounted Items, Desks, Secretaries, File Cabinets, Vanities, Metal Cabinets

Kitchen Items

  • Cooking Utensils
  • Dishes and Flatware
  • Drinking Glasses
  • Pots, Pans and Baking Dishes

We cannot accept:

  • Chipped Dishes, Stemmed Wine Glasses, Items with Logos, Used Water Bottles, Pressure Cookers

Linens

  • Bed Pillows without stains
  • Blankets, Comforters, Sheets, (NO Electric Blankets)
  • Mattress Pads (NO Mattress Toppers)
  • Towels and Washcloths

Small Appliances

Appliances and electronics must appear on our list

  • Air Fryers
  • Blenders, Food Processors, Mixers
  • Can Openers
  • Clocks and Clock Radios
  • Coffee Grinders
  • Coffee Makers (Drip, K-Cup Only)
  • Electric Kettles and Fry Pans
  • Fans and Space Heaters
  • George Foreman Type Grills
  • Instant Pots/Multi-cookers
  • Irons and Ironing Boards
  • Juicers
  • Lamps (NO Halogen, Wall or Ceiling Mounted)
  • Microwaves (Countertop only)
  • Rice Cookers
  • Slow Cookers
  • Sewing Machines
  • Toasters and Toaster Ovens
  • Vacuum Cleaners
  • Waffle Irons

We cannot accept any other appliances or electronics

Large Appliances

Appliances and electronics must appear on our list

    Must be newer than 10 years old

    • Refrigerators
    • Washing Machines and Electric Dryers
    • Air Conditioners up to 18″x 30″ x 24″

    We cannot accept any other appliances or electronics

    Electronics

    Appliances and electronics must appear on our list

    • DVD and Blu-Ray Players
    • Streaming Devices
    • Radios
    • Flat Screen TVs with Attached Base – (Less than 55”, Less than 10 years, Must have power cord)

    We cannot accept any other appliances or electronics. No Stereos or Music Systems of Any Kind – Radios Only

    Baby Items

    Must meet current safety standards and must be less than 10 years old*

    • Bassinets
    • Bed Rails
    • Car Seats *(Less than 5 years or at least one year before expiration)
    • Changing Tables
    • High Chairs
    • Infant and Toddler Seating
    • Infant Bath Tubs
    • Pack ‘n Plays
    • Safety Gates
    • Strollers
    • Toddler Beds and Mattresses

    We cannot accept:

    • Recalled Items
    • Monogrammed Items
    • Activity Centers and Seats, Cribs
    • Crib Bumpers
    • Cradles, Carriers
    • Bike Seats
    • Jumpers
    • Playpens
    • Swings
    • Toys
    • Walkers

    Home Furnishings

    • Curtains and Curtain Rods
    • Framed Art (up to 48”)
    • Home Décor Items
    • Mirrors (up to 36”)
    • Rugs (up to 8′ x 10′)

    We cannot accept:

    Custom Treatments, Blinds, Shades, Unbound rugs and rug pads; Baskets, Candles, Candlesticks, Fireplace Tools, Grandfather Clocks, Holiday Items, Picture/Photo Frames, Punchbowls, Vases, Media, Wine and Magazine Racks

    Donation Drop-Off Hours

    Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays from 9am to noon

    530 Main Street, Acton

    We are not able to accept donations at any other times.

    Please check our calendar for any holiday closings.

    Limited Pick-Up Service

    Household Goods offers limited pick-up service staffed by volunteers. We pick up furniture and household items in Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury, and Westford. For details and to schedule your pick-up please click here.

    Pick Ups from ReMARKable Donations

    Marks Moving & Storage, the official mover of Household Goods, provides pick-ups in and outside our volunteer pick-up area, in as soon as 2 business days, and for a fee. Click the green Donation Pick Up button to get started with ReMARKable Donations.

    Donation Pick Up - Marks Moving

    Pick Ups from ReSupply

    ReSupply is a veteran-owned organization that provides pick-ups in and outside our volunteer pick-up area, in as soon as 2 business days, and for a fee. Click the orange Donation Pickup button on this page to get started.

    More Ways to Donate Goods

    Amazon and Target Wish Lists

    We have a continual need for all items, especially sheets, towels, housewares, pots and pans, lamps, and dishes. Please consider selecting something from our Amazon Wish List or Target Wish List and having it shipped to us.

     

    Host a Goods Drive

    Host a drive for goods through your company, community group, scout troop, club, sports team, or neighborhood group. Learn more here.

    Please note we have a drop box container for the local non profit More Than Words for books, DVDs, CDs, audio books, & video games located in the donation drop-off area. We also have an Epilepsy Foundation drop box for clothing donations located in the donation drop-off area. We offer this as a convenience for the community and ask that you help us continue to do so by not leaving your items outside the bins if they are full.

    If we do not accept your item or are out of your area, please check our list of other other organizations.

    To furnish 3,000 homes a year, Household Goods requires a steady supply of good quality furniture and household items. Even with donated goods and volunteer time, it takes $300 to turn one empty apartment into a real home. Please consider supporting Household Goods today.
