Donate Furniture, Beds, and Household Items to Household Goods
Household Goods provides a full-range of good quality donated furniture and household items, free of charge, to 3,000 families in need each year.  We owe this to the incredible support of our community of volunteers and people and businesses who thoughtfully donate furniture and household items that meet our guidelines.
Our volunteers understand and share the frustration when they are faced with turning away items donors would like us to take. To avoid this situation, we strongly encourage all donors to read the guidelines carefully. Some of them may surprise you, but they reflect 30 years of experience in the service of our mission.
To make sure that we can accept your items, please first check the guidelines, then double check that your items are clean, complete and in good working order. We cannot provide our clients with broken, chipped, torn, stained, incomplete, unusable or recalled items, and therefore cannot accept them.
To furnish 50 homes a week, Household Goods needs a steady stream of clean, usable:
dressers, tables, chairs, and other furniture;
mattresses and bed frames;
dishes, flatware, and kitchen items;
sheets, towels, and other bed linens;
and other household items.
Please see our donation guidelines below; [https://www.householdgoods.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Donation-Guidelines.pdf] download our guidelines here. Please note that we do not accept clothing, food or toys.
We thank you in advance for your support in helping us help our community.
What We Can Accept
Click on each of the categories below to see what we can and cannot accept.
All items must be clean, complete, and in good working condition. We cannot afford to take and dispose of broken, chipped, torn, stained, incomplete, unusable or recalled items, or any item we deem unsuitable for our clients. Please [https://www.householdgoods.org/contact] contact us if you are uncertain about whether we will accept your item.

Limited Pick-Up Service
Household Goods offers limited pick-up service staffed by volunteers. We pick up furniture and household items in Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury, and Westford. For details and to schedule your pick-up [https://www.householdgoods.org/donate/donate-goods/pickup] please click here.
Pick Ups from ReMARKable Donations
Marks Moving & Storage, the official mover of Household Goods, provides pick-ups in and outside our volunteer pick-up area, in as soon as 2 business days, and for a fee. Click the green Donation Pick Up button to get started with [https://www.remarkabledonations.org/] ReMARKable Donations.
 [https://www.remarkabledonations.org] Donation Pick Up - Marks Moving
Pick Ups from ReSupply
ReSupply is a veteran-owned organization that provides pick-ups in and outside our volunteer pick-up area, in as soon as 2 business days, and for a fee. Click the orange Donation Pickup button on this page to get started.
More Ways to Donate Goods
Amazon and Target Wish Lists
We have a continual need for all items, especially sheets, towels, housewares, pots and pans, lamps, and dishes. Please consider selecting something from our [https://www.amazon.com/hz/wishlist/ls/8JMJW30SKMRX?ref_=wl_share] Amazon Wish List or [https://www.target.com/gift-registry/gift-giver?registryId=6689eac0-6247-11f0-8918-43da993488c9&type=CHARITY] Target Wish List and having it shipped to us.
Host a Goods Drive
Host a drive for goods through your company, community group, scout troop, club, sports team, or neighborhood group. [https://www.householdgoods.org/volunteering/host-a-drive/] Learn more here.
Please note we have a drop box container for the local non profit [https://www.mtwyouth.org/] More Than Words for books, DVDs, CDs, audio books, & video games located in the donation drop-off area. We also have an [http://www.epilepsynewengland.org/mission] Epilepsy Foundation drop box for clothing donations located in the donation drop-off area. We offer this as a convenience for the community and ask that you help us continue to do so by not leaving your items outside the bins if they are full.
If we do not accept your item or are out of your area, please check our list of other [https://www.householdgoods.org/other-organizations-2/] other organizations.
To furnish 3,000 homes a year, Household Goods requires a steady supply of good quality furniture and household items. Even with donated goods and volunteer time, it takes $300 to turn one empty apartment into a real home. Please consider supporting Household Goods today.
