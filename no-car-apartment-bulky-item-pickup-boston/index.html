Every cell comes from the official page or the company's own page linked in its row, with the date we last checked it.

Quick answers

Who will carry a couch or mattress out of my apartment or down the stairs around Boston?Of the pickup options we list, these say on their own page (or the City's list) that they come inside or handle stairs: ReSupply donation pickup (Boston): Yes. Teams go up and down stairs; disassembly is available. Extra fees may apply. LoadUp junk removal: Yes. Full-service option removes items from inside (basement, attic, upstairs); curbside option also offered. Lug Away Junk Removal: Yes. Inside-home pickup offered at a higher price (as listed by the City). CC Junk Removal & Hauling LLC: Yes. Indoor removal included (as listed by the City). 1-800-GOT-JUNK? (piano and hot tub removal): Yes. It says it removes items from anywhere, with a two-person team doing all lifting and loading. Confirm the price and access details with them before you book.
Will the City come inside to pick up furniture, a mattress or an appliance?No, not per the official pages we cite: 19 of the 20 City or Town pickup programs we list for Boston, Brookline, Cambridge, Newton, Quincy and Somerville say curbside only, so the item has to be at the curb (or where trash is collected) on pickup day. The page for City of Somerville medical equipment pickup / Council on Aging donation (via 311) doesn't say either way; check with the City.
I live in a big apartment building. Can I use City bulky item or mattress pickup?It depends on the building size and who collects your trash. The official pages say: Boston (City of Boston curbside furniture collection): Addresses that receive City of Boston curbside trash collection. Large buildings (7+ units) should check the City's large-building page and their property manager. Boston (City of Boston curbside mattress & box spring recycling (appointment)): City of Boston residents in buildings with 6 units or fewer. Buildings with more units must use their property manager or a private hauler. Cambridge (City of Cambridge mattress & box spring recycling (UTEC, $45 per item)): Cambridge residents. If your building has more than 12 units, the City says to ask your property manager first, because your building may have a different procedure. Newton (City of Newton bulky item curbside pickup ($20 per item)): Newton households on City trash collection. The City says businesses, institutions and many multi-family properties aren't entitled to City collection and must make private arrangements. Quincy (City of Quincy curbside furniture & bulky item collection): Quincy residents of one- to eight-family dwellings and condominiums. Businesses and apartment buildings with more than eight units must hire a private hauler; the City says it's illegal for them to use City curbside collection. Quincy (City of Quincy mattress pickup (South Shore Recycle, free for eligible residents)): Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer (free pickup, per South Shore Recycle). Somerville (City of Somerville curbside furniture & bulky item collection): Somerville addresses that receive City trash collection. The City says buildings with more than 6 units that use private trash service are not covered by its mattress program; for furniture, check with your building manager if you have private trash service. Somerville (City of Somerville mattress & box spring recycling ($55 sticker + 311 appointment)): Residential households in buildings with 6 or fewer units that receive regular City trash collection. Buildings with 7+ units or private trash service are not eligible.
Can I get rid of a bulky item without a car?Yes. Besides City curbside pickup, we list 24 charity pickups, furniture banks, haulers and other programs that come to you. 5 of them say they will come inside or handle stairs; for the others, access is limited or not stated, so ask when you book.
