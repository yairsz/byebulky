How to get rid of a couch, furniture or appliance in Quincy

Quincy picks up two pieces of furniture a week at the curb without a call, schedules mattresses separately through South Shore Recycle (free for most homes), and collects appliances and TVs if you call its hauler ahead. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

Does Quincy have bulky item pickup?Yes. Two bulky items per week, such as sofas, chairs, recliners and lawn furniture, can go out curbside on your regular trash day without calling. It covers one- to eight-family homes and condos.
Who is Quincy's trash company?Capitol Waste Services, Inc. collects Quincy's curbside trash and recycling for one- to eight-family homes and condos, Monday through Friday, per the City. Report a missed pickup at (855) 533-3400. It isn't contracted for move-outs or clean-outs, so hire a private vendor for those.
How do I schedule mattress pickup in Quincy, MA?Call South Shore Recycle at (617) 837-1299 or book online. It's free for Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer, and it's no longer collected on trash day.
How do I get rid of a refrigerator, washer or TV in Quincy?Call Capitol Waste at (855) 533-3400 by 3 p.m. before your trash day: at least one full business day ahead for most appliances, two for refrigerators, TVs and monitors.
What can I drop off at the Quincy DPW Yard?Appliances (no refrigerators or dishwashers), scrap metal such as desks, bed frames and exercise equipment, electronics, recycling, textiles and books at 55 Sea St. It's for Quincy residents with a MA driver's license.
What large items won't Quincy pick up at the curb?Toilets and construction or demolition debris, move-out and clean-out loads, oil-based paint and tires. Apartment buildings with more than eight units must hire a private hauler.
What day is bulky item pickup in Quincy?Furniture & bulky items: No call needed for furniture; it goes out on your regular weekly trash day (check the City's Interactive Curbside Collection Map for your day). Mattresses & box springs: South Shore Recycle advertises next-day pickup in many areas; exact wait not stated. Fridges & big appliances: Call by 3 p.m. at least one full business day before your regular trash day (Monday pickups call the Friday before).
Does bulk pickup take mattresses in Quincy?Not with regular bulky item pickup. Quincy's furniture pickup listing names this as not accepted: "Mattresses (separate scheduled pickup through South Shore Recycle, see below)." The official mattress option is City of Quincy mattress pickup (South Shore Recycle, free for eligible residents). Cost: Free for Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer. When: South Shore Recycle advertises next-day pickup in many areas; exact wait not stated. Not accepted by that program: Anything not placed outside: South Shore Recycle says it does not collect from inside units, hallways or common areas.

City bulk item pickup for Quincy residents (3)

city bulk pickup

City of Quincy curbside furniture & bulky item collection

Takes
  • Furniture: sofas, chairs, recliners and lawn furniture (two bulky items per week)
  • Carpet, cut into 3-foot lengths, rolled and tied (no more than 5 rolls at a time)
  • Microwave ovens (with regular trash)
  • Small amounts of wood, bundled and tied, 1 to 3 inches thick and under 3 feet long, in a barrel under 50 lbs
Not accepted
  • Mattresses (separate scheduled pickup through South Shore Recycle, see below)
  • Appliances, TVs, computer monitors, grills, lawn mowers and snow blowers (separate call-ahead pickup, see below)
  • Toilets and construction or demolition debris (do not place curbside)
  • Trash or debris from move-outs or clean-outs of rooms, apartments, basements or garages
  • Oil-based paint, tires and other hazardous items
Condition rules
Furniture is collected curbside without calling. Have it out by 7:00 a.m. on your pick-up day, and no rubbish may go out more than one day before collection (state sanitary code). The City notes that curbside collectors are not required to lift anything over 50 lbs.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside collection only.
Lead time / hours
No call needed for furniture; it goes out on your regular weekly trash day (check the City's Interactive Curbside Collection Map for your day). Holidays push that day and the rest of the week back one day.
Cost
No fee stated for furniture on the City's pages, check with them.
Service area
Quincy residents of one- to eight-family dwellings and condominiums. Businesses and apartment buildings with more than eight units must hire a private hauler; the City says it's illegal for them to use City curbside collection.
How to book
Website / booking · (855) 533-3400 (Capitol Waste Services, the City's hauler: missed pickups and questions)
Notes
Capitol Waste Services, Inc. performs Quincy's curbside collection Monday through Friday, per the City.

city bulk pickup

City of Quincy mattress pickup (South Shore Recycle, free for eligible residents)

Takes
  • Mattresses (all common sizes, including memory foam, hybrid, latex and drained waterbed mattresses, per South Shore Recycle)
  • Box springs and bed foundations, including platform bases and adjustable base frames (per South Shore Recycle)
Not accepted
  • Anything not placed outside: South Shore Recycle says it does not collect from inside units, hallways or common areas
Condition rules
Schedule first; the City says mattresses are no longer picked up on your regular trash day. Place the mattress at the curb or a designated outdoor spot before the scheduled pickup.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. South Shore Recycle says all pickups are curbside only and it does not enter homes or buildings.
Lead time / hours
Scheduled "dependent on vehicle and staff availability" (City). South Shore Recycle advertises next-day pickup in many areas; exact wait not stated.
Cost
Free for Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer. South Shore Recycle charges $60 per unit for larger Quincy buildings (8+ units) and non-Quincy residents.
Service area
Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer (free pickup, per South Shore Recycle).
How to book
Website / booking · (617) 837-1299 (number the City lists); South Shore Recycle's own site lists (617) 842-2434, Mon-Sat 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. · Info@SouthshoreRecycle.com
Notes
The City's pages and South Shore Recycle's site list different phone numbers, so try the other if one doesn't answer. Massachusetts has banned mattresses from the trash since November 1, 2022.

Furniture banks for Quincy residents (1)

furniture bank

Household Goods (Acton)

Takes
  • Standard-size mattresses & box springs
  • Bed frames (all parts) & bunk beds
  • Dressers & armoires
  • Couches, sofa beds & futons
  • Chairs (no office chairs)
  • Kitchen, dining, end & coffee tables
  • Refrigerators, washers, electric dryers (newer than 10 years)
  • Air conditioners up to 18" x 30" x 24" (newer than 10 years)
  • Flat-screen TVs under 55" (less than 10 years old)
Not accepted
  • Mattresses with any stains or rips
  • Office furniture, desks, file cabinets
  • Glass-top items
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Oversized or wall-mounted items
Condition rules
Clean, complete, and in good working condition; nothing broken, chipped, torn, stained, or recalled.
Stairs / inside pickup
Volunteer pickup asks that items be in a garage or on the first floor if possible.
Lead time / hours
Drop-off Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays, 9 a.m. to noon. Volunteer pickups typically scheduled 4+ weeks ahead. Paid pickups through ReSupply or ReMARKable Donations in as soon as 2 business days.
Cost
Drop-off: not stated. Volunteer pickup: minimum $35 donation requested. Paid pickups (ReSupply / ReMARKable Donations): for a fee.
Service area
Volunteer pickup only in Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury and Westford, so not Quincy. Quincy donors can drop off in Acton or use the paid pickup partners (ReSupply or Mark's Moving/ReMARKable Donations).

paid hauler
