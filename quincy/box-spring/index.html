How to get rid of a box spring in Quincy

A box spring can't go out with Quincy's regular trash. The City sends mattress and box spring pickups to South Shore Recycle, which is free for Quincy residents in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer. Here's how to book it, what it takes, and what to do if you're in a bigger building or need someone to carry it out.

Quick answers: box spring in Quincy

Does Quincy pick up box springs?

Yes. South Shore Recycle lists box springs and bed foundations of all sizes, including platform bases and adjustable base frames. The City of Quincy says to call South Shore Recycle at (617) 837-1299 or book online; mattresses and box springs are no longer picked up on your regular trash day.

Source: South Shore Recycle: what we take · City of Quincy: how can I get rid of

Is Quincy box spring pickup free?

Yes for Quincy residents living in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer (South Shore Recycle's "Free Quincy Resident Pickup"). For larger Quincy buildings with 8+ units and for non-Quincy residents, South Shore Recycle charges $60 per unit.

Source: South Shore Recycle: contact

Can I put a box spring out with the trash in Quincy?

No. The City says mattresses are no longer picked up on your regular trash day; schedule South Shore Recycle instead. Mattresses are also listed among items excluded from Quincy's curbside furniture collection.

Source: City of Quincy: how can I get rid of · City of Quincy: waste and recycle

Will South Shore Recycle take a box spring from inside a Quincy apartment?

No. It says all pickups are curbside only and it doesn't collect from inside units, hallways or common areas, so the box spring has to be outside before pickup.

Source: South Shore Recycle: residential

How do I get rid of a box spring from a big apartment building in Quincy?

South Shore Recycle charges $60 per unit for larger Quincy buildings with 8+ units. The City also says apartment buildings with more than eight units must hire private disposal for trash service. ReSupply's Boston page lists Quincy among communities it serves for paid in-home donation pickups that can include mattresses and box springs, and LoadUp's Quincy price list shows mattress removal from $120 with loaders carrying items out from any room.

Source: South Shore Recycle: contact · City of Quincy: waste and recycle · ReSupply Boston · LoadUp Quincy

Why a box spring can't go in Quincy's trash

The City of Quincy says mattresses are no longer picked up on your regular trash day and must be scheduled with South Shore Recycle, dependent on vehicle and staff availability. Mattresses are also excluded from the City's curbside furniture collection (two bulky items per week). The City's waste pages cover one- to eight-family dwellings and condominiums; apartment buildings with more than eight units must hire private disposal.

City pickup through South Shore Recycle: free if you qualify

Call South Shore Recycle at the City's listed number, (617) 837-1299, or schedule online through the City's linked booking form. South Shore Recycle's own contact page lists (617) 842-2434, Monday to Saturday 7 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Info@SouthshoreRecycle.com, so try the other number if one doesn't answer. Its "Free Quincy Resident Pickup" is available for Quincy residents living in homes, condos or buildings with 8 units or fewer. For non-Quincy residents or larger Quincy buildings with 8+ units, it charges $60 per unit. Its pricing page also shows mattress pickup starting at $60.

What South Shore Recycle takes, and the curbside-only rule

South Shore Recycle lists standard and low-profile box springs of all sizes, split box spring sets, platform bases and supports, adjustable base frames and wooden mattress foundations, along with twin through California king mattresses. All pickups are curbside only: place the box spring at the curb or a designated outdoor spot before the scheduled pickup. It does not enter homes or buildings and does not collect from inside units, hallways or common areas.

Need it carried out of the house?

ReSupply runs paid in-home pickups that include mattresses and box springs, with starting prices of $19, $49 and $99 plus a separate booking fee, and its Boston page lists Quincy among the communities it serves. LoadUp's Quincy page (checked Nov 6, 2026) says junk removal starts at $105 and its Quincy price list shows mattress removal from $120; LoadUp's mattress page says its loaders carry items out from any room, and curbside pickup takes $5 off. Confirm the final price when you book.

Donating a box spring

Household Goods in Acton accepts standard-size mattresses and box springs but refuses mattresses with any stains or rips, and its volunteer pickup doesn't reach Quincy, so you'd drop off in Acton or use its paid pickup partners. The Habitat for Humanity Greater Boston ReStore lists mattresses and box springs among items it does not accept.
