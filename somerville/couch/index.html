How to get rid of a couch or sofa in Somerville

If your building gets City trash collection, the cheapest way to get rid of a couch in Somerville is to put it at the curb on your regular trash day: the City picks up two pieces of furniture per week for free. If the couch is still in good shape you can donate it, and if you need it carried out of an upstairs apartment you'll need a paid hauler.

Quick answers: couch in Somerville

Does Somerville pick up couches?

Yes. The City picks up two pieces of furniture per week, free, at the curb with your regular trash. Put it out after 4 p.m. the day before and by 7 a.m. on collection day, without blocking the sidewalk.

Source: City of Somerville: trash and recycling

How much does it cost to get rid of a couch in Somerville?

City curbside furniture pickup is free. LoadUp's Somerville price list shows furniture removal from $120, with the final price shown by ZIP before booking.

Do I need a sticker or appointment to throw out a couch in Somerville?

No sticker is listed for furniture. Stickers apply to mattresses and box springs ($55 each, plus a 311 appointment) and large appliances ($25).

Source: City of Somerville: trash and recycling

Will someone take a couch out of my apartment in Somerville?

Not the City, which collects at the curb only. LoadUp says its loaders carry items out from any room, including upstairs.

Source: City of Somerville: trash and recycling · LoadUp

Where can I donate a couch near Somerville?

The Habitat Greater Boston ReStore in West Roxbury takes upholstered furniture that isn't ripped, worn, broken, dated or faded, but not sofa beds, futons or sofas with slip covers. Household Goods in Acton takes couches, sofa beds and futons that are clean and in good condition.

Free City curbside pickup: two pieces a week

The City of Somerville picks up two pieces of furniture per week on your regular trash day; its 2026 Trash and Recycling Guide calls it a free service. Look up your trash day by address on the City's trash page. Items can go out after 4 p.m. the day before and must be out by 7 a.m. on collection day, and you shouldn't block the sidewalk. Tape any mirrors or glass on both sides, and tie down or remove drawers and doors. Collection is delayed one day after City holidays. For questions, call 311 or 617-666-3311.

Private trash service buildings

The service covers Somerville addresses that receive City trash collection. If your building uses private trash service, check with your building manager before putting a couch out.

Sofa beds and futons

A sofa bed is still furniture, but futons are handled differently: the City's mattress program says futons are not part of the $55 mattress sticker program and go in regular trash. Mattresses and box springs need the $55 sticker and a 311 appointment.

Paid couch removal from inside your home

LoadUp says its loaders carry items out from any room (bedroom, basement, attic, upstairs) and also offers a curbside option. Its Somerville page says junk removal starts at $105, with additional items from +$15, and its Somerville price list shows furniture removal from $120 (add-on from +$30). Confirm the price for your ZIP when you book.

Donating a sofa in good condition

The City encourages residents to donate gently used furniture to area charities. The Habitat Greater Boston ReStore, 1580 VFW Parkway in West Roxbury, accepts upholstered furniture that isn't ripped, worn, broken, dated or faded, but not sofa beds, futons or sofas with slip covers; drop-off is Tuesday to Saturday, 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., and in-home pickup is booked through ReSupply, a paid service. Household Goods in Acton accepts couches, sofa beds and futons that are clean, complete and in good working condition; its volunteer pickup doesn't reach Somerville, so drop off at 530 Main Street on Tuesdays, Thursdays or Saturdays from 9 a.m. to noon, or use its paid pickup partners. The Salvation Army's satruck.org advertises free donation pickup of furniture; local terms are not stated, so check with them.
