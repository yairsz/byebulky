How to get rid of a couch, furniture or appliance in Somerville

Somerville picks up furniture for free with your trash, but mattresses, box springs and big appliances each need a paid sticker first, and mattresses also need a 311 appointment. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

11 options · every listing links to its source · last verified 2026-10-27

Does Somerville bulk pickup take mattresses?Yes, but not for free and not with the free furniture pickup. Since February 1, 2026, each mattress and each box spring needs its own $55 Disposal Sticker, and you must contact 311 by 3:30 p.m. the day before collection to schedule it. Unscheduled mattresses put out with trash are left on the curb and may be fined.
Does Somerville pick up furniture?Yes. The City picks up two pieces of furniture per week for free, curbside with your regular trash. Tape any glass or mirrors and tie down or remove drawers and doors.
Where does Somerville's recycling go?To the Casella facility in Charlestown, where the City says items from the blue recycling carts are sorted and recycled. If your trash or recycling is missed, the City says to report it no later than 3:30 p.m. the day after your scheduled collection.
How do I get rid of a fridge, washer or air conditioner in Somerville?Buy a $25 White Goods Sticker for each appliance and place it curbside without blocking the sidewalk. Take the door off a refrigerator first.
Does Somerville pick up TVs?Yes, for free. Call 311 to schedule a curbside pickup of a TV or computer monitor, or drop it off at the DPW Yard, 1 Franey Road. TVs with broken glass aren't accepted.
What large items won't Somerville take?Exercise equipment, exercise bikes, metal cabinets, building materials such as toilets, sinks and windows, motorized lawn mowers, snow blowers and car parts must be disposed of privately.
What day is bulky item pickup in Somerville?Furniture & bulky items: Your regular weekly trash day (look it up by address on the City's trash page). Mattresses & box springs: Pickup is one day after your regular trash day (Friday trash households: same day, Friday). The City page we checked doesn't give a day for fridges & big appliances; ask the City.
Does bulk pickup take mattresses in Somerville?Not with regular bulky item pickup. Somerville's furniture pickup listing names this as not accepted: "Mattresses and box springs (separate $55 sticker program, see below)." The official mattress option is City of Somerville mattress & box spring recycling ($55 sticker + 311 appointment). Cost: $55 per mattress and $55 per box spring (fee in effect since February 1, 2026). When: Pickup is one day after your regular trash day (Friday trash households: same day, Friday). Not accepted by that program: Futons, mattress pads and toppers (put in regular trash); Infant sleeping pads not meant for cribs; Excessively torn, soiled or moldy items; Broken, crushed or twisted items; Air beds and water beds; Bed-bug-infested mattresses (call 311 first; see notes).

City bulk item pickup for Somerville residents (5)

city bulk pickup

City of Somerville curbside furniture & bulky item collection

Takes
  • Furniture (up to two pieces per week)
  • Rugs and carpets (rolled, tied, roll no longer than 3 ft)
  • Bicycles
  • Wooden ladders and metal ladders (broken down to pieces no longer than 4 ft)
  • Wooden doors (cut in half)
Not accepted
  • Mattresses and box springs (separate $55 sticker program, see below)
  • Large appliances (separate $25 white goods sticker, see below)
  • Exercise equipment, exercise bikes and metal cabinets (heavy metal items: private disposal)
  • Building or construction materials such as toilets, sinks and windows (unless a building permit with the green sticker is displayed)
  • Motorized lawn mowers, snow blowers, aboveground metal pools, car parts
Condition rules
Free service limited to two items per week; place curbside with your regular trash. Tape any mirrors or glass on both sides, and tie down or remove drawers and doors. Bins and items can go out after 4 p.m. the day before and must be out by 7 a.m. on collection day. Don't block the sidewalk.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside only.
Lead time / hours
Your regular weekly trash day (look it up by address on the City's trash page). Collection is delayed one day after City holidays.
Cost
Free (the City's 2026 Trash and Recycling Guide calls it a free service).
Service area
Somerville addresses that receive City trash collection. The City says buildings with more than 6 units that use private trash service are not covered by its mattress program; for furniture, check with your building manager if you have private trash service.
How to book
Website / booking · 311 or 617-666-3311

city bulk pickup

City of Somerville mattress & box spring recycling ($55 sticker + 311 appointment)

Takes
  • Mattresses of all sizes, king to crib (foam, spring, mixed material, latex)
  • Box springs (all)
Not accepted
  • Futons, mattress pads and toppers (put in regular trash)
  • Infant sleeping pads not meant for cribs
  • Excessively torn, soiled or moldy items
  • Broken, crushed or twisted items
  • Air beds and water beds
  • Bed-bug-infested mattresses (call 311 first; see notes)
Condition rules
Buy one $55 Disposal Sticker per item (a mattress and a box spring are two items), then contact 311 to schedule no later than 3:30 p.m. the day before collection. Put the sticker on each item, place it curbside after 4 p.m. the day before and by 7 a.m. on pickup day, on public property, with nothing on top, leaving at least 36 inches of sidewalk clear. Limit: 2 bulk items per property per week. Unscheduled mattresses put out with trash are left on the curb and may be fined. Cancel by 12 p.m. the day before.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside only; the crew may not enter private property.
Lead time / hours
Pickup is one day after your regular trash day (Friday trash households: same day, Friday). Online stickers can take up to 10 days to arrive by mail, and you can't schedule until you have the physical sticker. Buying in person avoids the mail wait.
Cost
$55 per mattress and $55 per box spring (fee in effect since February 1, 2026).
Service area
Residential households in buildings with 6 or fewer units that receive regular City trash collection. Buildings with 7+ units or private trash service are not eligible.
Address
Stickers in person: DPW, One Franey Road (check, credit card or money order; no cash) or City Clerk, City Hall, 93 Highland Avenue
How to book
Website / booking · 311 or 617-666-3311
Notes
If the mattress is soiled or you suspect bed bugs, the City says to contact 311 for special instructions before buying a sticker. Its trash page says bed-bug-infested mattresses and box springs go out with regular trash, wrapped in plastic and clearly marked.

furniture bank

  • Air conditioners up to 18" x 30" x 24" (newer than 10 years)

Volunteer pickup only in Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury and Westford, so not Somerville. Somerville donors can drop off in Acton or use the paid pickup partners.
