About ByeBulky & how we verify

What this site is

ByeBulky is a directory of the ways to get rid of a couch, mattress, box spring or large appliance in one Boston-area city at a time. Every option is a real organization or a City program, not a category: a specific charity, a specific drop-off site, a specific hauler, and the City or Town program itself. For each one we record what it accepts, what it won't take, its condition rules, whether it collects from inside or only curbside, how long it takes, what it costs, and who it serves. Pages are organized two ways: a hub per city, and an item guide per city for mattresses, couches and appliances.

How a listing gets here

  1. We start with the official page. That means the organization's own website (its donation guidelines, service page or price list) or a government page (a City's Public Works or 311 page, a Town flyer, a state agency page such as MassDEP or Mass Save). Where a City publishes a list of haulers, the City's list is the source for what the City says about that hauler, and we label it that way.
  2. We read the page, not a summary of it. Each field is filled from the wording on that page. A statement on a City list is not treated as the company's own statement.
  3. We save the page. A copy of every page used is kept, so a claim can be checked against what the source said on the date we read it.
  4. We link the source. Every listing ends with numbered links to the page or pages it came from, and the date we last checked them.

What "Not stated, check with them" means

Plenty of sources say what they take but not what it costs, how long pickup takes, or whether the crew comes inside. When a source doesn't say, the field says Not stated, check with them rather than carrying a guess or a number from another company. The same applies to fees: a cost is shown only when the organization, or the City's official list, publishes one, and "no fee is mentioned" means exactly that, not that the service is free. Where a source's own wording and another page disagree, the listing's notes say which page says what instead of picking the tidier answer. 69 listing fields on this site currently read Not stated, check with them.

How often listings are re-checked

Every listing carries the date it was last verified. The dates currently run from 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-06 (14 distinct dates), so listings were checked at different times rather than all at once. A reported error is re-checked against the source right away, and a listing's date changes only when its source has actually been read again. City and Town rules change more often than a charity's donation list, so a City program is the first thing we re-check after a policy change or a new flyer.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish reviews, ratings or rankings, and we don't test or use the services. "Best" does not appear as a verdict because we haven't measured anything.
  • We don't take payment for placement, and no organization pays to be listed or to appear above another.
  • We don't collect quotes by phone and present them as published prices. If a hauler quotes only by phone or photo, the listing says so.
  • We don't cover cities we haven't researched. If a city isn't listed, it's because we haven't verified its rules yet, not because there are no options.

How we make money

ByeBulky is free to use. It is built to earn money later through affiliate or referral partnerships, for example a commission if someone books a paid junk pickup through a partner link. Right now ByeBulky has no affiliate relationships at all: there are no affiliate links on this site, no partner booking widget, and no link here earns a commission or carries an affiliate or tracking code. If that changes, every affiliate link will be labeled where it appears, this page and the privacy policy will say so before it goes live, and paid options will never push a free City or charity option off the page. A "Book a paid junk pickup" slot exists in the site's template for that day; it is empty and shows nothing today.

Analytics

ByeBulky uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to collect aggregate traffic and usage statistics, such as which pages are visited, so we can see what's useful. It doesn't collect your name or email, though Google and Microsoft may set cookies to tell visits apart. Details are in our privacy policy.

Corrections

Rules change often, and a wrong price or a closed drop-off costs someone a wasted trip. Every listing has a "Report a change" link that opens an email with the listing's name in the subject. You can also write to developer@2handedcoding.com and say which page and which line is wrong. We re-check it against the source, correct the listing, and update its verified date; if the source has changed its policy, the whole page gets another pass.
