How to get rid of a couch, furniture or appliance in Cambridge

Cambridge collects one piece of furniture a week for free with your trash, but appliances need a paid permit and mattresses are booked separately with the City's contractor, UTEC, for $45 each. Here's what each option takes, what it costs, and where we got it.

Does Cambridge pick up furniture?Yes. Each household can put out one piece of bulky waste, such as a couch, table or chair, per week with regular trash collection, free. Metal furniture is banned from the trash; metal desks and filing cabinets need a free permit.
Who collects trash in Cambridge?The City does. Cambridge Public Works collects trash, recycling, food waste and yard waste from most residential buildings, per the City. Report a missed pickup after 4 p.m. on your collection day or before 9 a.m. the day after, or call Public Works at 617-349-4800. For construction and other waste, the City publishes a list of private haulers.
How much is Cambridge bulky item pickup for appliances?$25 per item, or $20 if you're 62 or older, for items like air conditioners, washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves and TVs over 20 inches. Items aren't scheduled until the fee is paid.
How do I schedule mattress pickup in Cambridge?Book online with UTEC at utecinc.org/mattress-recycling. It costs $45 per mattress and $45 per box spring, and the City says slots fill up quickly.
Is there a drop-off for large items in Cambridge?The City says there are no drop-off locations in Cambridge for mattresses. Its Recycling Center at 147 Hampshire Street takes small electronics and scrap metal, but not appliances.
What can't go in the trash in Cambridge?Large appliances, metal items such as filing cabinets and furniture, mattresses, textiles, recyclables, yard waste, mercury items and non-alkaline batteries are banned from Cambridge trash.
What day is bulky item pickup in Cambridge?Furniture & bulky items: Your regular weekly trash day (look it up with the Get Rid of It Right tool or the Zero Waste Cambridge app). Mattresses & box springs: UTEC says Cambridge curbside pickups happen weekly and online requests can be made at any time. Fridges & big appliances: The City says most items are scheduled for Wednesdays or Thursdays.
Does bulk pickup take mattresses in Cambridge?Not with regular bulky item pickup. Cambridge's furniture pickup listing names this as not accepted: "Mattresses and box springs (banned from trash; separate UTEC program)." The official mattress option is City of Cambridge mattress & box spring recycling (UTEC, $45 per item). Cost: $45 per mattress and $45 per box spring (City mattress page, updated July 20, 2026). When: UTEC says Cambridge curbside pickups happen weekly and online requests can be made at any time. Not accepted by that program: Excessively torn, punctured, soiled or moldy items; Items infested (or previously infested) with bed bugs or other pests; Futons, mattress pads and toppers; Infant sleeping pads, air beds and water beds; Couches, bed frames, appliances and other furniture or textiles.

Fees and conditions vary; check each listing.

City bulk item pickup for Cambridge residents (3)

city bulk pickup

City of Cambridge curbside furniture & bulky item collection (no permit)

Takes
  • Furniture such as a couch, table or chair (one piece per week; not metal)
  • Carpet or lumber (residential only, up to 3 tied bundles, 3' x 3' x 1')
  • Toilet or sink (one per household on collection day)
  • Small TVs and computer monitors (20" or smaller screen, placed close to the curb edge)
  • Small electronics such as microwaves, stereos, box fans, small printers or fax machines
Not accepted
  • Metal furniture and other metal items such as filing cabinets (banned from Cambridge trash; metal desks and cabinets need a free permit)
  • Large appliances (permit and fee required, see below)
  • Mattresses and box springs (banned from trash; separate UTEC program)
  • Textiles such as clothes, sheets and towels (banned from trash)
  • Commercial or industrial items
Condition rules
Each household may put out one piece of bulky waste per week with trash collection. The City says there are some exceptions and points to its Get Rid of It Right tool to check an item. Items go curbside after 6 p.m. the night before or by 7 a.m. on collection day. Violations of the Refuse & Litter Ordinance can be fined up to $300 per violation per day.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside only.
Lead time / hours
Your regular weekly trash day (look it up with the Get Rid of It Right tool or the Zero Waste Cambridge app). Collection runs one day behind after a holiday.
Cost
Free (the City's curbside brochure says one piece of furniture per week, free of charge).
Service area
Cambridge residential buildings that get City trash collection. The City's reuse page says the weekly item is for households whose trash the City collects.
How to book
Website / booking · 617-349-4800 (DPW) · theworks@cambridgema.gov
Notes
The City's trash page says one piece per household per week; its older curbside brochure says one piece per building per week. If you share a building, check with DPW.

city bulk pickup

City of Cambridge appliance & large item pickup (permit required)

Takes
  • Air conditioners and dehumidifiers (Freon removed)
  • Clothes washers and dryers
  • Refrigerators and freezers (non-commercial; doors removed)
  • Stoves
  • TVs and computer monitors over 20"
  • Copiers and printers over 40 lbs
  • Trash compactors, water coolers and water heaters
  • Larger motorized exercise equipment
  • Free-permit items: small exercise equipment, bicycles, lawnmowers, radiators, snow blowers, metal filing cabinets and desks
Not accepted
  • Commercial or industrial items
  • Mattresses and box springs (scheduled separately with UTEC)
Condition rules
Every item on these lists needs a permit before pickup, even the free ones. The City says an item isn't on the collection schedule until all fees are paid. Remove refrigerator and freezer doors. Place items curbside by 7 a.m. on your collection day, or after 6 p.m. the night before.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside pickup.
Lead time / hours
Prescheduled. The City says most items are scheduled for Wednesdays or Thursdays. Exact lead time not stated.
Cost
$25 per item, or $20 if the resident is 62 or older, per the City's permit application page. Free permit for bicycles, metal desks and cabinets, grills, exercise equipment, lawnmowers and snowblowers. Paying online adds a convenience fee ($0.99 + 2.99% by card, or $5.50 by e-check); paying in person avoids it.
Service area
Cambridge residents.
Address
Permits in person: DPW, 147 Hampshire Street (Mon 8:30 a.m.-8 p.m., Tue-Thu 8:30 a.m.-5 p.m., Fri 8:30 a.m.-12 p.m.; check, money order, cash or credit card)
How to book
Website / booking · 617-349-4800 · theworks@cambridgema.gov
Notes
Fee and convenience-fee amounts are from the City's online permit application page (cambridgema.viewpointcloud.com), which loads with JavaScript; the main City page lists which items need a paid or free permit but not the amount.

city bulk pickup

City of Cambridge mattress & box spring recycling (UTEC, $45 per item)

Takes
  • Most mattresses
  • Box springs
Not accepted
  • Excessively torn, punctured, soiled or moldy items
  • Items infested (or previously infested) with bed bugs or other pests
  • Futons, mattress pads and toppers
  • Infant sleeping pads, air beds and water beds
  • Couches, bed frames, appliances and other furniture or textiles
Condition rules
Schedule online with UTEC, the City's contractor, and schedule early because the City says slots fill up quickly. Put items at the curb in front of your address by 7 a.m. on collection day, and not before 6 p.m. the night before. The City suggests taping a note with "UTEC", your order number and pickup date to each item. UTEC's limit is 8 pieces per pickup. To cancel for a full refund, UTEC says to email at least 24 hours before the pickup date; a no-show item is still charged.
Stairs / inside pickup
No. Curbside only; the crew is prohibited from coming onto private property.
Lead time / hours
UTEC says Cambridge curbside pickups happen weekly and online requests can be made at any time. The crew picks up before 3 p.m. on your date. Exact wait not stated.
Cost
$45 per mattress and $45 per box spring (City mattress page, updated July 20, 2026). A $5 discount is available for financial hardship; call UTEC at 978-856-3997.
Service area
Cambridge residents. If your building has more than 12 units, the City says to ask your property manager first, because your building may have a different procedure.
How to book
Website / booking · 978-856-3997 (UTEC) · mattress-recycling@utecinc.org (problems: copy recycle@cambridgema.gov)
Notes
The City's separate large-item page still says mattress collection is free; the City's dedicated mattress page, updated more recently (July 20, 2026), lists the $45 fee. Massachusetts has banned mattresses from the trash since November 1, 2022.
